# Kill brief: PSD straight-alpha source-over

- Lock: `razul-psd-alpha`.
- Trigger: formats audit finding 5 at `ec979743` showed that fallback compositing omits destination-alpha weighting and straight-alpha normalization, then premultiplies the wrong RGB again at publish.
- Scope: `psd_import.rs::over_pixel`, direct tests, and the kill report.

## Required fix

Use straight-alpha Porter-Duff source-over with `dst_a` and `out_a`:

`rgb = (blended * sa + dst_rgb * dst_a * (1 - sa)) / out_a`

for `out_a > 0`. Preserve blend-mode behavior, zero-coverage no-op, and alpha rounding.

## Tests

- Half-alpha red over transparent produces straight RGB 255, alpha 128, and premultiplies to 128 at publish, not straight 128 then premultiplied 64.
- Half-alpha red over half-alpha blue has correctly normalized RGB and `out_a = 0.75`, not a darkened double-scaled result.
- Existing opaque blend tests remain unchanged.

## Constraints and verification

No PSD feature expansion, release, upstream PR, or centralhost. Run focused tests, full `psd_import` tests, full app if needed, rustfmt, clippy with `-D warnings`, and independent review before commit.
