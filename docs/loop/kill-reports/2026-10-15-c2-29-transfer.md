## Finding (pinned by a test)

The transfer ray runs INWARD from `surface + n * front_offset`.
`umber-cli`'s default `offset = 0.0` means HIGH detail outside the low
surface sits behind the origin and is never hit; the cage front then does
nothing. The panel starts rays at the cage (`front_offset = front`).
Mutation check: offset 0 makes the GPU test miss (alpha 0). The CLI's
default has the same behavior, but changing it is a CLI decision, not
made here.
