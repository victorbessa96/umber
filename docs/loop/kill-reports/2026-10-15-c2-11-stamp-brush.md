## Built

- **`BrushParams.stamped`** (bool) **and `.stamp_angle`** (`StampAngle`),
  both `#[serde(default)]`, so pre-stamp `.umberbrush` files load radial
  and unrotated. A test loads the old JSON shape and round-trips the new
  one.
- **`StampAngle`** has four modes:
  - `None` (the default);
  - `Fixed { degrees }`;
  - `FollowStroke { offset_degrees }`: the dab turns to the direction
    travelled from the previous stamp in the segment, and the first dab
    of a segment has no travel and stays at the offset;
  - `Random`: an FNV-1a hash of the dab position, deterministic, so a
    replayed stroke rotates identically.

  Non-finite degrees read as 0.
- `DabAdapter` (build-up and wash paths) emits `FALLOFF_STAMP` dabs with
  their angle when `stamped`, and the old radial dabs otherwise
  (`dab_for`).
- **The stamp is session state, not params:** `PaintState::set_stamp /
  clear_stamp / stamp_size` go through the compositor every target
  shares. Params stay `Copy`, and a preset names *that* it is stamped,
  not the pixels.
- `PaintCompositor::set_stamp` / `clear_stamp` now take `&self` (the
  stamp sits behind an `RwLock`): the compositor is `Arc`-shared across
  a session's paint threads. `splat_dabs` holds one read guard for its
  whole recording, so a splat can never sample a stamp swapped
  mid-record. Still `Send + Sync` (the pool test).
- The four struct literals outside `dab_adapter` (preset, library, brush
  panel, paint_state test) gained the fields.

## Tests

- `stamped_params_emit_stamp_dabs_with_the_angle_mode`:
  - radial by default;
  - Fixed 90° gives π/2 on every dab;
  - FollowStroke gives 0 on the first dab, 0 for +x travel and π/2 for
    +y.
- `random_angles_are_deterministic_and_spread`: same position, same
  angle; 64 positions cover at least 6 of the turn's quarter-radian
  buckets; NaN gives 0.
- `pre_stamp_params_json_loads_radial_and_unrotated` and the round trip.
- **`a_stamped_brush_paints_the_stamp_shape`** (GPU, ran): through the
  real `PaintState`, a left-half stamp plus a stamped vertical stroke
  paints left of the stroke and leaves right of it empty. The same
  stroke radial paints both sides.

  **Mutation check:** ignoring `stamped` in the adapter fails it at
  "right half empty under the stamp".

  The first draft painted nothing: a lone begin+end emits no dab,
  because the conditioner needs a run of events. The control assert
  caught it, and the test now drives a short stroke, as the
  `drive_converged_diagonal` precedent does.
- Gate 1: umber-gpu 145, umber-brush 146, umber-paint 162, umber-app 492.
  fmt and clippy clean. The `paint::` gpu module passes 15/15 under
  `--features gpu`, and gpu-feature clippy is clean. The first battery
  run read RED from a stale fingerprint: the tar copy kept old mtimes, so
  umber-gpu was not rebuilt. Touching the files cleared it, and the
  lesson is now in the skill.
