**Finding (U3-5):** `BrushPreset.alpha_curve` and `radius_curve` are edited in the Brush panel ("Pressure → alpha", "Pressure → radius") and saved, but nothing in the paint path reads them: the only consumers are `validate()` and the editor. Pressure reaches dabs only through `params.pressure_curve` (after `pressure_gamma`) into alpha in `DabAdapter::stamps_to_dabs*`; radius never varies with pressure. Two UI controls that do nothing. The per-property pressure-curve item becomes: wire `radius_curve` into the dab radius and fold `alpha_curve` with `pressure_curve` (or retire one), pinned by a dab test. That is an umber-brush/umber-paint engine change, its own kill.

| U3-5 pressure curves wired | `BrushParams.pressure_maps: PressureMaps { flow, size }` (`#[serde(skip)]`: the file keeps one copy as `alpha_curve`/`radius_curve`). `BrushPreset::engine_params()` folds the preset's curves in; the app syncs the paint session with it instead of the bare `params`. `DabAdapter`: flow re-maps the shaped alpha after `pressure_curve` (both build-up and wash); `dab_for` scales the radius by `size(pressure)`. Identity curves short-circuit EXACTLY (no float re-evaluation), pinned bit-identical by `identity_pressure_maps_are_bit_identical`; the paint goldens (umber-paint 185/185) are untouched. Behaviour change by design: the four shipped presets with non-identity alpha curves (Airbrush, Eraser Soft, Grunge, Soft Round) and Pressure Round's size curve now do what their files always said. Panel labels localized: "Pressure → flow" / "Pressure → size". | `umber-brush/src/dab_adapter.rs`, `preset.rs`, `preset_library.rs`, `umber-paint/src/paint_state.rs` (test literal), `main.rs`, `brush_panel.rs`, `assets/i18n/*.ftl` |
