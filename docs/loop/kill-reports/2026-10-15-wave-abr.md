# Kill report (UNRUN, host-gated): .abr brush import (ROADMAP wave-abr)

Date: 2026-10-15. Author: dragon. Lock: `wave-abr` over
`brush_panel.rs`, the new `brush_panel/abr_import.rs`, and this report.
Requirements §1: "Brush presets (.abr import optional; native preset
files)". The ROADMAP calls `.abr` "the one Adobe format that is legal".

## Written

- **Parser: ag-psd 0.3.0 `read_abr`**, already a dependency of
  umber-app (PSD import; MIT), so there is no new crate and no lock
  change. Versions 6/7/9/10 with minor 1/2; 1/2 are refused by ag-psd.
- **`brush_panel/abr_import.rs`**, a submodule of the panel, so no
  `mod` line in the locked `main.rs`:
  - sampled brush → stamped `.umberbrush` plus a `<stem>.stamp.png`
    tip (white on transparency, which `stamp_coverage` reads back as the
    tip's own alpha);
  - computed brush → radial preset with its hardness;
  - every brush keeps its size (`diameter / 48`, Umber's base diameter),
    spacing (Photoshop % of diameter → `1 / (2 × spacing)` dabs per
    radius) and a fixed angle;
  - dynamics, scatter, texture, dual brush, colour dynamics, transfer,
    wet edges and noise are NOT carried, and are reported per brush.
    Erodible/airbrush/bristle tips are skipped with a reason.
- **Never-crash:**
  - a 64 MiB file cap;
  - a 5000 px tip cap (Photoshop's own maximum);
  - `catch_unwind` around ag-psd;
  - a **preflight walk** of the `samp` and `patt` sections, refusing
    hostile sizes before ag-psd allocates them (see below).
- **Panel:**
  - "Import .abr…" writes into the user preset dir, never overwriting
    (a taken name gets ` 2`), reloads the shelf, selects the first brush,
    and shows the report;
  - selecting a stamped preset loads its `.stamp.png` into the session
    once per selection (`load_stamp_for_active`); a preset without a tip
    leaves a hand-picked stamp alone.

## The ag-psd allocations, closed by the preflight

ag-psd 0.3.0's `read_abr` builds its reader with no memory budget
(`total_memory_limit: None`, `reader.rs:208`), so three allocations take
their sizes from the file. The preflight walks the sections first and
refuses:

| Allocation | Where | Unchecked, up to | Preflight cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| a `samp` tip `vec![0; w*h]` | `abr.rs:962` | 4 GB | 5000 px per side |
| a `patt` name `with_capacity(units)` | `reader.rs:568` | 8 GB | units × 2 ≤ its record |
| a `patt` bitmap `vec![0; w*h*4]` | `reader.rs:3092` | 3.6 GB at ag-psd's 30000² box cap | ≤ 256 MiB of RGBA |

All three caps were checked in a Python port: the 100000² tip and the
30000² pattern are refused, and a 4×4 tip and a 512² pattern pass. The
right long-term fix is a budget inside `read_abr` upstream (ag-psd).

## Tests

`abr_import::tests`:
- sampled → stamped, with exact tip bytes, size 2.0 from 96 px, 2 dabs
  per radius from 25%, a fixed 30°;
- computed keeps hardness; wet edges reported;
- missing / oversized / short tips and bristle shapes are skipped with
  4 report lines; garbage bytes and the 100000² preflight are typed
  errors;
- write → re-write gives ` 2`, the stamp PNG round-trips through
  `stamp_coverage`, and a dotted name still finds its tip.
